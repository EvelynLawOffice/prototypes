Housing Law

Skilled representation to landlords in eviction and housing matters throughout Massachusetts. Attorney Evelyn understands the challenges property owners face and works to enforce landlord rights while ensuring compliance with the law. Landlords deserve strong, fair advocacy to protect their investment and maintain their rental property.

  • Filing and prosecuting eviction cases
  • Drafting, reviewing, and enforcing lease agreements
  • Recovering possession of rental property
  • Negotiating tenant settlements to avoid unnecessary delays or costs
  • Advising on landlord–tenant law compliance
  • Court procedures
